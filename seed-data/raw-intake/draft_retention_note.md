## 1. Draft Proposal

This note captures an early proposal for shortening customer CRM retention from
seven years to five years, pending legal and governance review. It has not gone
through the department's standard sign-off process yet, and no data owner has been
assigned to it.

## 2. Open Questions

- Whether a shorter retention window is compatible with existing regulatory hold
  requirements is still unresolved.
- Whether anonymized-at-expiry satisfies the purge requirement, or whether full purge
  is required, needs a legal reading before the draft advances.
- The payroll and billing systems' downstream retention dependencies have not been
  surveyed.

## 3. Status

This draft should not be treated as an approved policy position until it is reviewed,
an owner is assigned, and it passes the exception process in POLICY-DG-055. It is
retained in the corpus only as an example of an unowned draft document, the ABAC
manifest intentionally marks it restricted with no data owner, so it is never
returned to demo personas outside the data-governance team.
