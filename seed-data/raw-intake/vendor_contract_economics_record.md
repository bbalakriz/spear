## 1. Purpose

This record documents the actual negotiated commercial terms Strategic Sourcing
agreed with Northbridge Logistics Partners, an approved vendor under purchase
order PO-2026-0442, as part of the 2026 contract renewal. It exists so Finance
and Legal can reference the real negotiated economics without pulling the
signed master agreement every time a renewal, audit, or benchmarking exercise
comes up.

## 2. Negotiated Contract Economics

These are the actual terms Northbridge Logistics Partners agreed to in the
signed master agreement, not the vendor's published rate card, and not what
any other logistics vendor on the current panel receives:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Rebate structure | Tiered volume rebate, retroactive once the annual threshold is met |
| Negotiated rebate rate | 18.5% off list rate |
| Minimum annual volume commitment | $2,400,000 |
| Exclusivity clause | None, Northbridge is a non exclusive logistics provider |

## 3. Classification

These figures are classified **Restricted** under POLICY-DG-042, the
competitively sensitive commercial terms tier, not the personal data tier.
Disclosure outside Strategic Sourcing and Legal weakens this company's own
negotiating position at the next renewal and would breach the master
agreement's own mutual confidentiality clause (Section 12), since this same
rebate structure is negotiated with other vendor customers and Northbridge
would have every incentive to walk the rate back once it knows what a peer
account pays.
This is not a fraud exposure the way account numbers are, it is a competitive
one.

## 4. Review

Reviewed at each contract renewal cycle, or immediately if Strategic Sourcing
renegotiates any term in this table, per POLICY-DG-042 section 5's change
verification requirement for commercially sensitive contract data.
